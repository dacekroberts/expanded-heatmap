# Data sources — Australia

<!-- internal -->The Australia part of the project's provenance record,

| Melbourne | **The City of Melbourne's Census of Land Use and Employment (CLUE)**, "Business establishments with address and industry classification" - one row per establishment the census counted, with its trading name, address, an ANZSIC4 class (a few finer than ANZSIC's own: convenience stores, clothing and footwear by wearer) and the property's point | All three buckets via `pipeline/taxonomies/anzsic_fes.py`, Sydney's class list plus CLUE's six finer retail classes.
