## Transit feeds

| City | Agency / system | Endpoint | Retrieved | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oslo | Ruter via **Entur**, Norway's national access point — **T-bane 1–5 and trams 12, 13, 15, 17, 18, 19** | `https://storage.googleapis.com/marduk-production/outbound/gtfs/rb_rut-aggregated-gtfs.zip` | 2026-09-24 | 50,999,897 bytes, keyless. ⚠️ **HEAD is not a probe here** — Google Storage answers 200 with size 0; a ranged GET returns 206 and `PK`. ⚠️ **Every route type is EXTENDED** (`401` T-bane ×5, `902` tram ×6, `1008` ferry ×6, buses 7xx) — a basic-type reader sees no rail. ⚠️ **`feed_info.txt` names Entur and declares NO window**, so the fetch date pins the snapshot (Toulouse's case). Kommune only, trams drawn, ferries excluded — owner's calls; the 12 stations outside are all in Bærum. **Gate 3: 101 T-bane stations** (a secondary source, Norwegian Wikipedia); OSM's relations are too partial here (line 2: 25 stations vs the feed's 47). ⚠️ **The feed colours every T-bane line `EC700C` and every tram `0B91EF`**, so the per-line colours are Ruter's own as recorded on **OpenStreetMap**'s route relations (ODbL, notice 1), two lightened in hue to clear the colour check; tram 15 (line 12's route while the Briskeby line is closed) has none and takes this project's teal. Licence **NLOD** — **notice 29**, whose wording Entur specifies: "Data made available by Entur" + its logo (shown on the Oslo page, owner's call). Ruter's APP agreement bars copying "data som Ruter gjør tilgjengelig", but it is formed on installing the app and no data page incorporates it; read as governing the app, and recorded rather than buried | 
| Bergen | Skyss via **Entur** - **Bybanen lines 1 and 2** (`SKY:Line:1`, `SKY:Line:2`) | `https://storage.googleapis.com/marduk-production/outbound/gtfs/rb_sky-aggregated-gtfs.zip` | 2026-09-27 (cache) | 84.5 MB, keyless, NLOD - notice 29. ⚠️ **Bybanen is route type `901`**: Oslo's `401`/`902` filter draws nothing. 68 platforms -> 34 stop places -> **33 stations by name** (Bergen busstasjon is two places 70 m apart), all inside the kommune; 624 m median gap, so the standard rings. `feed_info.txt` names Entur and declares no window. **Gate 3 not run**: Wikipedia (en, no) gives 35 stops network-wide and no per-line split, against the feed's 34 places. No `route_color`, and OSM's relations give both lines #BF4525: line 1 keeps it, line 2 is #9c27b0, this project's choice. Byparken / Kaigaten (73 m) kept as two stations. Ferries not drawn |
