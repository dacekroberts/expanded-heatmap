| City | System / operator | Endpoint | Retrieved | Note |

| Copenhagen | **OpenStreetMap** — Metro **M1–M4** (`route=subway`, operators Inmetro / Metro Service A/S) and S-tog **A, B, Bx, C, E, F, H** (`route=light_rail`, operator DSB): 11 lines, **29 route relations** in the bbox before the whitelist | The three Overpass mirrors in `pipeline/osm.py`, bbox `55.40,12.00,56.00,12.70`, `out geom` on the relations and `node(r)` for their stops | 2026-09-24 | **Why not the agency: Rejseplanen's national GTFS exists, is keyless and current** (`https://www.rejseplanen.info/labs/GTFS.zip`, 54,971,494 bytes, modified 2026-09-21) **and was NOT downloaded**: its Labs guidelines grant CC BY 4.0 but ask users not to change the data so that it differs from the original, and document access as by request with those guidelines accepted. Neither point was resolved in this project's favour (owner's call 2026-09-24). ⚠️ **Every relation in the bbox carries `network=Takst Sjælland`** — the fare zone, Lokaltog and Movia's Letbane included — so the whitelist is **ref + route + operator**, never `network`. **S-tog is drawn on the published spacing-and-frequency test** (owner's call; Dublin's DART precedent): median in-scope spacing 1,227 m, every line every 10 minutes. Stations are the kept relations' `stop` members (all 283 are `public_transport=stop_position`), collapsed by name — eight names are Metro/S-tog interchanges, widest Nordhavn at 279 m. **Gate 3 exact**: Metro 15/16/17/13 = 44 (English Wikipedia's line table, secondary) and S-tog 87 (DSB's own page, which also says 86). Colours are the relations' own, S-tog A and F lightened against the shared ΔE floor. OpenStreetMap, ODbL 1.0 — notice 1 and the rail-geometry notice |

| Copenhagen | **OpenStreetMap kommune relations** (`boundary=administrative`, `admin_level=7`), polygonised from their outer AND inner member ways — Kobenhavn is relation 2192363, Frederiksberg 2186660 | The three Overpass mirrors in `pipeline/osm.py`, bbox `55.40,12.00,56.00,12.70`, `out geom` — 38 relations, 35 Danish | **Kobenhavn (`ref` 101) + Frederiksberg (147)**, owner's call 2026-09-24: **94.4 km²** and **8.7 km²**, gated at 80-110 and 8-10. ⚠️ **Frederiksberg is an enclave, so Kobenhavn's relation carries it as an INNER ring** — a polygon from outer ways alone would put Frederiksberg's stations in Kobenhavn; step 1 asserts the overlap is 0.000 km². OpenStreetMap, ODbL 1.0 — notice 1 |
