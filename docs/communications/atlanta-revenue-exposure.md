# Atlanta: a note to the City about its 2024 business-licence layer — DRAFTED 2026-10-01, FILED, NOT TO BE SENT

**Status: drafted and filed on the owner's instruction (2026-10-01): "we can
draft this but file it, unlikely to send".** It is not outreach for data. It
would tell the City about a likely unintended exposure. Only the owner sends
it, from their own address, if they ever choose to.

**Why it exists:** DECISIONS 2026-09-29, "Atlanta's 2024 licence layer is
not a publication to build on".
- **What the layer is.** The City's planning GIS account publishes
  `BusinessLicenses_2024_Revenue` (19,297 rows), shared to Everyone. It
  sits outside the City's open-data hub and declares no licence.
- **What it carries.** `PREVIOUS_YEAR_REPORTED_REVENUE` on 19,264 rows, and
  an employee count.
- **Why that matters.** O.C.G.A. § 48-13-15 makes gross-receipts
  information given to a local government for occupation tax confidential.
- **Same fields elsewhere.** The account's 2019–2023 yearly layers carry
  the same fields.

**The project's own position is unchanged:** Atlanta stays discarded on
terms. Revenue and employee figures are never published from any source. No
revenue value appears in this note or anywhere in the repository.

**To (untested addresses):** `gis-team@atlantaga.gov`, or the named contact
on the City's open-data hub. Before sending, the owner should confirm the
right office: the City's GIS team, or the Office of Revenue that the layer
names as its source.

**Subject:** Business licence layer on ArcGIS Online appears to include reported revenue

Hello,

While looking at public business-licence data for a non-commercial mapping
project, I came across an ArcGIS Online layer published by a City of
Atlanta account, "BusinessLicenses_2024_Revenue". It is shared publicly and
described as "Business Licenses for 2024. Source: Office of Revenue". It
includes a field named PREVIOUS_YEAR_REPORTED_REVENUE, filled for nearly
every business, and an employee count. Earlier yearly layers on the same
account appear to carry the same fields.

I may be misreading it, but gross-receipts information reported for
occupation tax is, as I understand it, treated as confidential under
O.C.G.A. § 48-13-15. So I wanted to let you know in case the layer's public
sharing is unintended. I have not used, kept or shared any of the revenue
figures, and our project does not publish anything from this layer.

No reply is needed. Thank you for the City's open data work.

[Owner's name]
