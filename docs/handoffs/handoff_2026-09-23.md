## Three patterns this session produced, worth carrying

**1. 🚨 A route relation with no `ref` appeared in FOUR cities in one day** —
Bucharest's `Extensie M4`, Singapore's `JRL` (5 relations, uncoloured),
Stockholm's unref'd subway *and* tram, and 27 of Seoul's 65 commuter
relations. **A `ref`-keyed build drops them silently; a count-keyed build
draws a line it cannot label.** **This belongs in `osm-rail` as a raising
check, not in a fifth city's notes** — which is that skill's own opening
argument.

**2. In DCAT, the property you want is on the CHILD.** Göteborg's `accessURL`
*and* its licence were both on the distribution, not the dataset — and the
dataset node carries **no licence property at all**, so a reader checking it
would record the city as SILENT and be **wrong in the permissive direction**.

**3. The checks framework has no way to express "expect a non-200", and I
tried three times in one day** — `http_status_in`, `http_status_expected`, and
a deliberately-failing `http_ok` whose claim said *its failure was the
finding*. **That last one is not a check, it is a note that breaks the suite**,
and a suite that always shows a failure teaches people to ignore failures.
Facts of that shape go in prose.
