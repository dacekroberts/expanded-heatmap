### Method notes

- The universe is Wikipedia's "List of tram and light rail transit systems"
  (Europe section plus Türkiye from its Asia section) and "List of metro
  systems" (list and "Under construction"), read as wikitext on 2026-10-03,
  plus satellite towns the systems serve. One row per city served; a few
  satellite groups are one row.
- Each city was matched case-sensitively, with word boundaries, on its native
  spelling, diacritic-free forms and English exonyms, against
  `docs/city_master_list.md`, `city_master_list_evidence.md`,
  `city_master_list_2026-09-30.md`, `city_master_list_history.md`,
  `global_country_shortlist.md`, `commuter_rail_list.md`,
  `tram_city_list.md` and `app/cities.py`, then against every other `docs/`
  file, `DECISIONS.md` and `PLAN.md` as a fallback. `docs/build_briefs/` holds
  no brief for any never-recorded city. Hits were read in context; a bare
  mention (a feed listing, a parent line in a sibling's row) is classed
  "never screened", not recorded.
- Kazakhstan's Almaty and Astana are left to the Asia agent.
