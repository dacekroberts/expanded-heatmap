# Privacy verdicts, one per city

`CLAUDE.md`: run `python scripts/check_personal_exposure.py <city>` before
publishing a city, and record the verdict in `DECISIONS.md`. This table points
at each city's record, so `scripts/check_privacy_verdicts.py` can require one:
it fails when a built city has no row, a verdict is not one of the values
below, or the cited entry is not in `DECISIONS.md` or `docs/decisions/` or
never names the city. **A new city adds its row when its verdict is recorded.**

- `publish` - the exposure check was run and the city published on its result.
- `publish-structural` - the pins show no person-name field of any kind (the
  source has none, or step 2 never loads one), so there is nothing to expose.
- `pending` - a known open question for the owner, reported by the check
  rather than failed; it names the entry that states the question.

Built 2026-10-01 from a reading of every city's entries (review lesson 1 of
`docs/review_lanes_2026-09-30.md`). Where a city was re-checked, the row cites
the latest verdict. Several cities' records state the check's numbers without
a verdict word; they are `publish`, as published on those numbers.
