## Retiring a worktree

`docs/session_roles.md`, steps 1-4. Each step was learned the hard way.

1. Confirm the branch is merged, the tree is clean, and the owning session is
   **not running**. Ask that session directly: the `cwd` that `list_sessions`
   reports does not show where a session is actually working.
2. List reparse points over the **whole** folder, and remove each junction on
   its own, non-recursively: `[System.IO.Directory]::Delete(path, $false)`.
   `data\france\raw` is a junction to the shared 3 GB SIRENE pair, and a
   recursive delete can follow it.
3. Copy any `data/<city>/` the main checkout lacks, then `git worktree remove`
   and `git branch -d` (never `-D`).
4. "Filename too long" comes from the worktree's `.venv-lean`: re-scan, then
   `cmd /c rmdir /s /q "\\?\<path>"`. An idle session that is still open
   locks its now-empty folder until the owner closes it.

## Rules that kept paying

- Keep a positive control, and check each negative case for its **expected
  message**, not just its exit code.
- Measure a claim in the rendered artifact, not in the file.
- Before a soft reset or squash, merge the newest master **first**. A reset
  onto a master that has moved silently reverts the commits you didn't have.
- Verify a peer's "facts" yourself before acting on them.
