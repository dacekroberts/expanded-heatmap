### 2026-10-07 - Place search: the owner's answers; stations and lines searched as the map draws them, homes kept out, the index URL stamped with its hash (owner, branch)

- **"1,2 yes 3 is this considered a violation of any kind? ... 4 leave as reader set. 5 sounds good. 6. ... can we ensure station and line searches match the respective ui items we've added?"** (owner, this session's chat):
  - **1:** the wording approved as proposed.
  - **2:** `LICENSE` carries the carve-out as proposed: the place indexes under `app/static/places/` are ODbL 1.0 only, not MIT and not the reservation.
  - **3 (named markets):** answered in chat, not yet decided: no licence or indemnity issue (the index is OpenStreetMap's alone, and every indemnity the project accepted belongs to a register or a tile service); the question is the privacy line, since an OSM marketplace can be one company's market hall. Recommended: markets only when notable (wikidata or wikipedia, the tourism rule), and a scope line on what the search is for. Not in the index: amenity=marketplace was never in the query, so it needs one more Overpass query for Kyoto.
  - **4:** the rings stay as the reader set them; no help line added.
  - **5:** rollout as recommended (the Japanese cities, then the US and Canadian grid cities, then the rest).
  - **6, built:** stations and lines are read from the map itself (the station markers' tooltips, the legend's line rows and each line's polylines), so a result carries the name the map shows and a line result picks its line out as its legend row does. OSM's station entries are joined in the browser to the nearest drawn station within 300 m with the same core name or the same first word of 3+ letters, then by a name it already holds, and lend it their Japanese and alternate names; a same-named OSM station further out (within 1,500 m) is a stray duplicate and dropped; any other is labeled "Station not on this map". Nothing of the map's own data enters the published index (ODbL 4.4(d)). Measured on Kyoto: all 117 drawn stations and all 18 drawn lines come first when searched by their names on the map; 四条大宮 finds Shijō-Ōmiya, 西院 finds Saiin (Randen's "Sai" joined through Hankyu's), "Kyoto Station" finds Kyoto. Loosely, any shared word joined the scenic line's "Torokko Saga" to Saga-Arashiyama; the first-word rule keeps them apart. A line's Japanese name (烏丸線) finds nothing as a line: the map shows English line names only.
- **Homes out of landmarks:** a condominium's grounds (tagged leisure=park on landuse=residential) was a landmark. Any landmark on residential land, in a house or apartment building, or with private access is dropped unless notable (4 in Kyoto); the index holds no name with マンション, ハイツ, レジデンス, コーポ or アパート.
- **The index URL carries its content hash** (`place_index.index_url`, `?v=` and 12 hex digits): the browser kept the old file under the same URL and showed the condominium after it was removed. The map step reads the hash, so the index step runs first: `step4_place_index.py` renamed `step2b_place_index.py`.
- **The JS escapes:** the Edit tool had written the CJK and combining-mark ranges as literal characters; they are built in Python now (`_js_ranges`), so the source holds `\u` escapes (the no-escapes rule's hazard, met through a tool rather than a heredoc).
- **New wording for the owner** (not covered by answer 1): the result labels "Line" and "Station not on this map", and "Streets and places are searchable on the site's own map pages" for a map opened outside the site (replacing "Place search is available on the site's own map pages").

### 2026-10-07 - Place search piloted on Kyoto: a 1.04 MB index (270 KB gzipped) fetched on first use, searched in the browser; Tokyo estimated at 2.3 MB (branch, held for the owner)

- **Brief:** item 3 of "Four ideas assessed before the large review" (owner, 2026-10-07): places only, never business records; searched entirely in the visitor's browser over a static per-city index from OpenStreetMap; not Nominatim's public service. A pilot on a branch: nothing landed, nothing pushed.
- **Fetch:** one Overpass query for Kyoto, cut by the city's own boundary (`["name"="京都市"]["admin_level"="7"]`), answered first time by overpass-api.de: 81,517 elements, 11.5 MB, cached under a new name, `data/kyoto/raw/osm_places_2026-10-07.json`. Kyoto's steps 1 and 2 were not re-run; step 3 was re-rendered on the branch only. `pipeline/place_index_fetch.py <city>` is shared, not a per-city `fetch_sources.py` mode: the query is the same for every city and only the boundary filter (`config.PLACE_AREA`) differs. `check_no_fetch_in_steps.py` passes.
- **The index** (`pipeline/place_index.py`, run by `pipeline/kyoto/step2b_place_index.py`): 11,836 places, 1,041,065 bytes, 269,604 gzipped (Streamlit served 284,393 bytes at its own gzip level). By kind: 5,152 neighborhoods (the 町, 48% of the bytes), 2,639 landmarks, 2,424 street-pair intersections, 960 streets (8,861 named ways merged by name), 502 signed junctions (四条烏丸), 160 stations. Every name tag OSM carries is searchable: name, name:en, name:ja-Latn, the kana readings, alt and short names. A signed junction also gets the streets through it in both scripts ("四条通 / 烏丸通", "Shijo Street / Karasuma Street").
- **Places only, measured:**
  - Landmarks with a commercial tag (shop, office, craft, brand, cuisine, operator:type, an eating or lodging amenity) dropped: 49.
  - Tourism and education landmarks need a wikidata or wikipedia tag (308 dropped): without one, OSM's tourism=attraction and amenity=college held a tea-ceremony shop and a private computer school.
  - Gardens need wikidata, wikipedia or a heritage tag, and a residential garden never goes in (139 dropped): one untagged garden was named "Aoyama's Garden", possibly a person's own name at a home.
  - place=isolated_dwelling left out (Kyoto's one was an apartment building). Minor historic objects (memorials, wayside shrines, boundary stones, milestones, tombs; 3,179) left out as clutter unless notable.
  - One college with its own Wikipedia article stays (京都コンピュータ学院): notable by the rule above.
- **The search box** (`map_common.PLACE_SEARCH_SCRIPT`, opt-in by `render_heatmap(place_index_url=...)`, so every other map renders unchanged): a magnifier under the layer control. The index is fetched once, on first use, from `/app/static/places/kyoto.json` (Streamlit's static serving, turned on in `.streamlit/config.toml`), never embedded: the Kyoto map grows 12,906 bytes of script and no data. No `<form>` (a submit would put the text in a GET), spellcheck, autocorrect and autocapitalize off (a browser's cloud spellcheck sends typed text away), `credentials: "omit"`, `no-referrer`, no history entry, no logging. A pick fits a street's extent or zooms to 16 on a point and shows a ring marker for 12 s that taps pass through.
- **Matching:** NFKC, lowercase, accents stripped (Shijō = Shijo), Japanese long vowels folded both sides (shijou = shijo); a Latin word matches the start of a word or, at 4+ letters, the inside of a romanized compound; CJK matches anywhere; a CJK word that matches nothing is retried as two halves (烏丸四条); "station"/駅 match any station, street words are optional on streets. Own code rather than MiniSearch or leaflet-search: their whitespace tokenizers treat 四条烏丸 as one word, so 烏丸 would not find it, and no third-party script has to load. MiniSearch's prebuilt index (toJSON) was not measured.
- **Measured in a local render** (desktop Chromium, `localhost`, lean venv): index fetched and prepared in 138-148 ms; each keystroke 1.3-6.4 ms, 8-10 ms for a single Latin letter. Not measured on a phone.
- **Queries, 50 tried on the final build:** 47 found the place first or second (three, Kinkakuji, Ginkakuji and Nanzenji, only after joined-name matching was added for OSM's "Kinkaku-ji"), among them Shijo Karasuma, shijou karasuma, 四条烏丸, 烏丸四条, Karasuma Shijo, Karasuma and Oike, Sanjo Kawaramachi, Horikawa Imadegawa (OSM spells the street "Imaidegawa"; found through the junction's "Horikawaimadegawa"), Pontocho, 先斗町, Kinkakuji, Golden Pavilion, Ginkakuji, Nanzenji, Kiyomizudera, 二条城, Nijo Castle, Kamo River, Philosopher's Walk, Fushimi Inari, Kyoto Tower, Heian Shrine, からすま. Failures: Nishiki Market and 錦市場 (a marketplace, left out as commercial; an owner call below); Gion Shirakawa (an area known by two names, no OSM object carries both). "Kyoto Station" lists the bus terminal 京都駅前 (name:en "Kyoto Station") before the station 京都, 120 m apart.
- **Tokyo, estimated:** one count-only Overpass query over the 23 wards (23 ward relations matched): 20,931 named highway ways (3.65 times Kyoto's 5,739), 3,784 place nodes (0.73), 5,983 named junctions (9.35), 764 stations (4.66), 10,767 landmark candidates (1.52). Kyoto's measured bytes per kind, scaled: about 2.3 MB raw, about 0.6 MB gzipped, so about twice Kyoto's load and keystroke time.
- **Licence:** read by `licence-read` (ODbL 1.0 and the OSMF's Geocoding, Substantial, Produced Work and Attribution guidelines): PERMITTED WITH CONDITIONS. The index is a Derivative Database, served to browsers is Publicly Use and Convey; the file carries ODbL's notice and link in a `license` object, the search box shows ODbL §4.3's own wording with a link to download the file (§4.6), and nothing non-OSM goes in (§4.4(d)). Row in `docs/data_sources.md`, "Place search index". Open: `LICENSE` needs a carve-out (below).

  1. **The wording**, all new published text: the placeholder "Street, station, place"; the kind labels (Station, Intersection, Landmark, Neighborhood, Street); "No street, station or place by that name"; "Place search is available on the site's own map pages" (a map opened outside the site); the credit "Contains information from OpenStreetMap, which is made available here under the Open Database License (ODbL). Download this index". Recommended as written.
  2. **`LICENSE` carve-out**, proposed: "The place indexes under `app/static/places/` are databases derived from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, and are licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0 only (https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), not under the MIT License or the reservation above." Required before any index is published.
  3. **Named markets** (amenity=marketplace: Nishiki Market): recommended IN as places, since a market's name is a place people navigate by and no stall is listed; tradeoff: it is the one class of place that is a collection of businesses.
  4. **Rings after a jump:** the rings are off by default (owner, 2026-09-21), so a search lands on dots without the rings that answer the site's question. Recommended: leave the layer as the reader set it and add a line to "Using the map" on how to turn them on; the alternative is to switch the rings on at the first jump.
  5. **Rollout:** recommended the 34 Japanese cities first (the signed junction names and bilingual tags are where search helps most, and the Japanese name tags are what the matcher was tuned on), then the US and Canadian grid cities, where "5th and Pike" is the common query and the derived intersections answer it; the rest after. Each city is one Overpass query, so about 170 across several days, inside overpass-api.de's guidance (about 1 GB a day). Before rollout: a phone measurement on Tokyo's index, `fetch` recording the cache in each city's `provenance.json`, and `brief_check`-style checks that the index carries no row with a commercial tag.
