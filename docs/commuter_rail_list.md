# Commuter rail — which cities have it, and which maps draw it

**Snapshot, 2026-09-24**, after Copenhagen was built. A list to come back to,
not a settled policy — the policy is `docs/excluded_categories.md`, "Which
stations these maps are drawn around".

**The standing rule: commuter rail is excluded in every city.** Two built
cities are the exceptions, each kept as a decision with its alternative
recorded, because inside the city the service runs at metro spacing and
frequency. Rows marked *ASSERTED* rest on general knowledge, not on this
project's record — verify before relying on them.

## The pattern

Both exceptions were decided on **station spacing and frequency inside the
city**, the test `excluded_categories.md` states — not on who runs the trains.
**Japan, Seoul, Hong Kong and São Paulo will each need the same decision at
build**, because there commuter lines carry much of the city's own traffic.
Settle each one in its brief before the build reaches its rail step.
