# Commuter rail — which cities have it, and which maps draw it

**Snapshot, 2026-09-24**, after Brazil's nine cities were built. A list to come back to,
not a settled policy — the policy is `docs/excluded_categories.md`, "Which
stations these maps are drawn around".

**The standing rule: commuter rail is excluded in every city.** Five built
cities are the exceptions, each kept as a decision with its alternative
recorded, because inside the city the service runs at metro spacing and
frequency. Rows marked *ASSERTED* rest on general knowledge, not on this
project's record — verify before relying on them.

## The pattern

All five exceptions were decided on **station spacing and frequency inside the
city**, the test `excluded_categories.md` states — not on who runs the trains.
Brazil added the third part, **coverage**: how many of the line's stations have
no drawn station within walking distance. `scripts/measure_rail_backbone.py`
answers spacing and coverage from cached OSM; frequency stays a cited read.
**Seoul and Hong Kong will each need the same decision at build**,
because there commuter lines carry much of the city's own traffic. **Japan's
is taken: include** (owner, 2026-09-24).
Settle each one in its brief before the build reaches its rail step.
